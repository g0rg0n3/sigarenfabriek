Artist Bio

Marina van der Lecq started out as a restorer working for museums, went into digital fabrication, and graduated at DOGtime last year with explosions on paper when she tried to catch the right moment. Being too curious to leave it at that, she is now delving into cultural analysis studies.

With her love of craftsmanship and her love of the story, she creates work without focusing on any particular medium or technique.

‘I see my practice as a playing-together of material, tools, words, space and time. Together we construct, invent or fail. Failure not as malfunctions but as moments of potential. It shows that each of us could play a role in uncovering the immanent freedom that hovers in each system. It allows wonder, reflection or critique.

Description of Artwork #1

Does the body make the mind?

An installation featuring a Shakespearean sonnet with ASCII animation and a receipt printer

In ‘Does the body make the mind?’, you are confronted with an intelligent machine that wonders what it is like to have a living body. The question it indirectly poses is whether an intelligent machine can create consciousness? It cannot resolve this question, grows arrogant and claims the mind-body problem is irrelevant, before switching to the notion that language is power.

Language here changes form, from code to poetry and asci-image, from text to paper, from machine to experience. What do you, as a visitor, think of this issue? What happens to language when it touches a body? What can a machine control in this context, and what it cannot?

Description of Artwork #2

The Fire We Keep - center piece

A 10-metre-long leporello that will be filled during the exhibition with an extra-long cadavre exquis. A cadavre exquis is a collaborative drawing in which participants do not know what the others have drawn. In this mega-drawing at the exhibition, the artists reflect on what it means to be part of a collective. By contributing to the drawing, everyone helps to create a large body without anyone having a complete overview of what this body actually entails. During the finishage, the artwork will be unveiled and sold.
