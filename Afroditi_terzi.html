Artist Bio

Afroditi Terzi works primarily with sewing and painting, building surfaces in layers. Thread and paint exist like two voices in dialogue, supporting or interrupting each other.

Description of Artwork

“Blue”, 170x140cm, 2026

“Wings briefly”, 120x80cm, 2026

The two works are part of a larger new series, started in 2026. In this series, Afroditi explores the idea of ‘negative space’, discussing whether empty space is void or something active; a space that enables other spaces. Reflecting on loss, what could have been, and the quiet tension that exists in between.
