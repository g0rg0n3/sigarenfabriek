Artist Bio

As an artist, she explores the artistic value of the human body, delving deeply into its complexities and expressive potential. Her process is intuitive and deliberate, allowing for a blend of spontaneity and precision. Marjolijn’s art exists at the intersection of time, space, and human movement. Her work reflects a dynamic interplay between physical form and the concepts of temporality and spatiality. She is interested in how movement can convey emotion and narrative, inviting viewers to engage with her pieces. Through her work, she aims to challenge perceptions and encourage a dialogue about the body’s role in art and society.

Description of Artwork

THE FUTURE HUMAN PROJECT

Our anatomy changes incredibly slowly. A few million years ago, we were still walking on all fours. What will we look like in a few million years’ time? How are we influenced by technological developments? What effect do changes in our environment or our diet have? These drawings are part of a long-term research initiative. (Pencil on wood - 62W x 123H)

THE CROSS STOOL

During her Artist in Residence programme at the former Koepelgevangenis (prison) in Haarlem, she often focused on ‘sitting’. How does the body react in this environment? How do we experience freedom of movement or the passing of time? This ‘cross stool’, specially made for the residency, offers space to think, see and feel. (Available in Bamboo and Pine wood - Approx: 36L x 11,5W x 16,5H)
