Artist Bio

Lisa is a drawing-based artist working at the intersection of material research and intuitive making. She draws on raw cotton surfaces, often left unframed with exposed, cut edges that emphasize fragility and openness rather than containment. Using Japanese ballpoint pens alongside watercolour, she explores delicate contrasts between precision and fluidity. The choice of materials is essential to her practice, where surface, tool, and gesture are closely connected. Her work reflects vulnerability within both process and material presence. Rather than imposing form, she follows what the material suggests, allowing drawings to emerge through attention, silence, and responsiveness—allowing the viewer to feel her search.
