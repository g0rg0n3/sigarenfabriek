Artist Bio

Emily W. Bernstein is an interdisciplinary artist based in The Hague. Her work explores how memory, archives, and technological systems shape our understanding of lived experience and our role in narrating what resists representation. Working across installation, moving image, objects, and text, she investigates how systems of mediation reconstruct or unsettle seemingly stable narratives, foregrounding moments of rupture and instability. Her practice engages with the ethical and epistemological implications of these processes while remaining rooted in research-driven experimentation.

Description of Artwork

Work title: “Signal, clear, echo”

In this photographic series, Emily investigates traces of invisible rupture within systems. Inspired by tako-tsubo cardiomyopathy (broken heart syndrome), the work emerges from clay imprints shaped through pressure and containment. They trace the unstable boundary between internal states and external structures, approaching conditions that resist resolution or representation.
