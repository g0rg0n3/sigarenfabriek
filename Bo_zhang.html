Artist Bio

Bo Zhang (b.1988, Harbin, China) is a visual artist with a main focus on figurative painting. In her artistic practice, she uncovers subtle dramatic tensions within everyday life, often using her personal photo archive as a reservoir of fleeting moments to be translated onto the canvas. Zhang’s work seeks a delicate equilibrium between precise representation, imagination, abstraction, and an intuitive painting process. A central focus of her current work is the investigation of nature and animals within highly industrialized urban environments. She is particularly fascinated by the quiet friction between organic vitality and the rigid architecture of the modern world.

Description of Artwork

Title: Fluorescent Greenery

Material: oil on canvas

Dimension: three-part installation of 3 paintings, each 80 x 60cm, installation size around 80(H) x 210(W) cm

Inspired by an encounter in Utrecht’s old town, this series of three paintings explores the intriguing contrast between organic flora and an orderly, industrial interior. The work is marked by the contrast between a cluster of lively houseplants and a striking neon-red plastic chair viewed through an expansive glass facade. This series examines how we 'contain' and commodify nature, reflecting on how wild elements are often reduced to curated ornaments within modern urban life. By presenting three sequential viewpoints, the installation aims to reenact my movement past the space, highlighting subtle shifts in perspective and atmosphere to create a dynamic, immersive viewing experience.
