Artist Bio

Katarzyna Baldyga (b. 1988) grew up in the rural-industrial region of Silesia, Poland. She graduated in law in Wrocław in 2012 and worked in the Prosecution Service. Moved to the Netherlands in 2018, where she graduated from the 5 year program of DOGtime at the Gerrit Rietveld Academie. She travelled to Leipzig to engage with the New Leipzig School.

In 2024 she began a full-time artistic practice with studios in Amsterdam and Dresden. Her work, Searching for What Can’t Be Put Into Words, was acquired by Museum Voorlinden, while Starfish entered the Collectie Rijksoverheid. In 2024 she received the Ron Mandos Young Blood Award and spent a year at the Hochschule für Bildende Künste in Dresden with painter Helen Verhoeven.

Description of Artwork

I work through process-driven layered paintings and with provisional maquettes. My imagery is in constant flux, but materiality is central to my practice.

For me, paint, painting, and paintings are forces able to charge, open up, strangle, tell a story, bounce joyously, conceal and reveal, twist and surprise, change points of view, drip, fall apart, to be real and illusionary, complicated and simple, smooth and rough, go over, under and through.

Rebels, oil and tempera on canvas, 200x100cm. The work takes inspiration from the novel I Who Have Never Known Men by Jacqueline Harpman.

The female figures appear partially contained within the cropped frame. Unapologetically present, embedded in a shifting environment where narrative is suggested rather than explained. Their bodies merge with surrounding forces, evoking the image of untamed women.
