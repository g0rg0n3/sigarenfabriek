Artist Bio

Unveiling the invisible

Nicolas Dagieu is playing with the audience's imagination and the thin line between dream and reality.

His works questions our individual spectrum of belief as well as our positioning facing actual societal challenges. His multimedia approach lets him create on-site specific stories that bring the public to question the limit of their imagination.

Nicolas invites living-like creatures in his artwork as a tribute to the movement who create life, a poetic dance. Inspired by natural forces and mysterious historic stories, each artwork leaves to the audience a memorable anchor to the present moment and often, something invisible to bring home.
